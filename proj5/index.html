Part A: Power of Diffusion Models

Part 0: Playing with DeepFloyd

In Part A of this project, we will be playing around with pretrained diffusion models, specifically DeepFloyd IF, to generate images for tasks of our choosing (e.g. visual anagrams and hybrid images). To familiarize ourselves with the DeepFloyd IF diffusion model, we start by generating several images using prompts of our choosing.

Note: DeepFloyd takes in vector inputs, not the string prompts directly, so we must first obtain the prompt embeddings (from the T5 text encoder).
Prompts used:
  1. Prompt 1: “a photo of a person kayaking in a clear lake”
  2. Prompt 2: “an oil painting of a rainy city sidewalk during twilight”
  3. Prompt 3: “a photo of many plushies on a bed”

Image at num_inference_steps=20:

Image at num_inference_steps=80:

Image at num_inference_steps=120:

Overall, the images look relatively good.

For the kayak photo, the model's output displayed the "clear lake" portion of the prompt pretty realistically for steps 20 and 120 and gave the most realistic background in step 80. The kayak itself was a bit of an issue though - the paddles are incorrect in the first two images, and the person completely disappears in the third image.

For the rainy city sidewalk photo, the image at step 20 looked the most natural (step 80's image has a light hanging from... the sky?), but step 120 has more color, as if it was trying to capture the "during twilight" more.

For the plushies photo, I'd argue that the photo at step 80 was the most realistic - the faces in the other images look a bit too scary, and the background in step 120 doesn't really look like a bed.
