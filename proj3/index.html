Warping Images

Earlier, how did we apply our homographic matrix to our image? We considered two methods:

  • Nearest Neighbor Interpolation:

    Pros: faster, doesn't hallucinate (no new colors)

    Cons: can result in sharp edge artifacts

  • Bilinear Interpolation:

    Pros: better transitions (no unwanted sharp artifacts)

    Cons: slower, hallucinates

With homographies, we can also rectify images.
